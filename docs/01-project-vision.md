# 01 — Project Vision

## Product

**ORVIX Commerce** is positioned as an AI-powered ecommerce growth platform with expert-led marketplace operations — not a traditional digital agency.

The marketing website is the face of a software company. Services extend the platform; they are not the entire story.

## One-liner

> Your Complete Ecommerce Growth Partner — run, optimize, and scale across every Indian marketplace from one intelligent system.

## Core job of the website

Generate **qualified leads**.

Primary CTA: **Book Free Consultation**  
Secondary CTA: **Request Free Ecommerce Audit**

Every page ends in a conversion path. No dead ends.

## Visitor belief (success criteria)

Within seconds, the visitor should believe:

> “This company can run my ecommerce business better than I can.”

They should feel trust, expertise, premium quality, technology, scale, and reliability.

## Audience

| Segment | Job to be done |
|---|---|
| New sellers | Launch correctly without costly mistakes |
| Growing brands | Scale listings, ads, and ops without hiring a full team |
| Manufacturers / importers | Digitize catalog and sell online |
| D2C brands | Expand onto marketplaces without losing brand control |
| Enterprises | Multi-marketplace ops with governance and reporting |
| Startup founders | Speed + capital efficiency |

## Positioning pillars

1. **Technology-first** — ORVIX AI + ops dashboards as the product wedge  
2. **Experts second** — specialists execute with platform leverage  
3. **Marketplace depth** — Amazon → Flipkart → Meesho → fashion → quick commerce → ONDC  
4. **Outcomes** — revenue, health, retention — not activity theater  

## Competitive frame

Closer to Stripe / Linear / Vercel / Notion / Framer / Apple in craft than to a typical ecommerce “agency” site.

Do **not** copy SellerRocket or any peer. Match or exceed quality with an original identity.

## Brand personality

Modern · Confident · Premium · Minimal · Intelligent · Helpful · Trustworthy · Elegant · Fast · Professional  

Never loud. Never flashy. Never corporate-boring. Never cheap.

## Non-goals

- Looking like a generic agency template  
- Dumping 40+ services as one flat list  
- Animation for decoration  
- Hardcoding brand strings across pages  
- Building internal SaaS app UI as a real product (mockups/workflows only for marketing belief)

## Definition of done (site)

- Feels like a company valued at $100M+  
- Handcrafted, award-worthy craft  
- SEO-first, accessible (WCAG AA), Lighthouse 95+ targets  
- Single `config/brand.ts` rebrands the entire site  
- Docs in `/docs` are the source of truth for all implementation
