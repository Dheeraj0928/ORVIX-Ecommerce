# 06 — Copywriting

## Principles

- Benefit-driven, professional, confident  
- No fluff  
- Every block increases conversion or trust  
- Platform language first; services as capability of the platform

## Homepage messaging (draft)

### Hero
- **Brand:** ORVIX  
- **Headline:** Run every marketplace like a single, intelligent system.  
- **Sub:** ORVIX Commerce combines AI-powered operations with specialist teams — so your brand grows across Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, and beyond without the chaos.  
- **CTAs:** Book Free Consultation · Request Free Ecommerce Audit  

### Problem
Selling across marketplaces shouldn’t mean living inside twenty tabs, broken listings, and surprise account health hits.

### Why sellers struggle
- Fragmented ops across marketplaces  
- Listings that don’t convert  
- Ads that spend without learning  
- Inventory and returns bleeding margin  
- Compliance and account health as constant risk  

### How we solve it
One operating system for catalog, ads, inventory, compliance, and growth — powered by ORVIX AI, executed by experts.

### ORVIX AI (capabilities to feature)
Generate titles · descriptions · SEO keywords · product images · background removal · resize · A+ drafts · listing optimization · competitor analysis · pricing suggestions · inventory monitoring · catalog drafts · bulk listing management

### Process labels
Discovery → Strategy → Optimization → Growth → Scale

### Stats (use consistently until real data replaces)
- 500+ Brands Managed  
- ₹100M+ Revenue Managed  
- 98% Client Retention  
- 10+ Marketplaces

## Voice examples

**Good:** “Cut return rates with listing quality and ops discipline — not guesswork.”  
**Bad:** “We are a passionate team of ecommerce enthusiasts leveraging synergies.”
